Session 9 fix — voiced-adaptive minimum LPC guard order:
  chord_Cmaj_formant_max: 15.1 → 17.9 (+2.8)
  sine_440_formant_upmax: 30.1 → 32.8 (+2.6)
  For voiced+formant frames: Guard 3 min order raised from 2 → 8.
  This gives LPC 4 conjugate pairs to work with, helping polyphonic/sawtooth cases.
  For voiced+pitch-only frames: kept at min order 2 (higher order hurts pitch shift).
  WHY vocal formant cases barely improved: LPC at 48 kHz can't separate formants from
  pitch. All speech harmonics (120–3500 Hz) have ω << π, so short-lag autocorrelation
  r[1]/r[0] ≈ 1 regardless of order. The extra poles at orders 3–8 fit harmonic residuals,
  not vocal-tract formants. True fix requires pitch-synchronous LPC or cepstral liftering.
  sine_440_formant_downmax regressed -2.5: sawtooth is "voiced" (rich harmonics) AND has
  formant shift → gets minOrder=8 → 8-pole downshift less accurate than 2-pole.

Open: LPC formant capture for voiced speech at 48 kHz — fundamental limitation:
  vocal_aah_formant_downmax = 17.0; vocal_aah_pitch_up7st = 19.0
  The 48 kHz sample rate means ALL speech harmonics (f0=120 Hz to F4=3.5 kHz) have
  normalised frequency ω = 0.016–0.46 rad/sample. Short-lag autocorrelation r[k] at
  lags 1–8 is dominated by cos(ω) ≈ 0.9+ for all harmonics. Neither Guard 3 removal,
  pre-emphasis, nor minimum-order raising fixes this: the poles at order 3–8 fit
  harmonic residuals, not vocal-tract formants.
  Tried and reverted Session 8: Guard 3 removal → near-Nyquist pole aliasing.
  Tried and reverted Session 9: pre-emphasis (α=0.97) → doesn't change r[1]/r[0] at 48 kHz.
  Next architecture fix: pitch-synchronous LPC or cepstral liftering.

     that pre-emphasis and minimum-order changes can't fix vocal formant capture at
     48 kHz. The correct fix is pitch-synchronous analysis: analyse one pitch period
     (zero-pad to 1024 for resolution) or apply cepstral liftering to separate the
     spectral envelope (formants) from the harmonic fine structure (pitch).
     Expected improvement if fixed correctly: vocal_aah_formant_downmax 17.0 → ~28+,

  2. Transient-synchronous OLA for time compression — drum_hit_time_2x (19.4) and
     time_4x (18.8) are poor because onset cramming smears the transient; OLA phase
     reset at detected onsets would help.  V-Synth BACKING mode stores event timestamps
     at encode time (amplitude-peak onsets) for exactly this purpose.

  3. Sawtooth formant downshift — sine_440_formant_downmax regressed to 25.4 in v10b
     (-2.5 vs v8). Caused by the sawtooth being misclassified as "voiced speech with
     formant shift" (it has harmonic energy in 1-4 kHz band, same as speech formants).
     A stronger voiced-speech discriminant (e.g. modulation depth of formant peaks,
     or pitch-period detection) could route the sawtooth to minOrder=2.
