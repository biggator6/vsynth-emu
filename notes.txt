Routing (v18g: pitch→LPC now requires SOLO content type):
  hasFormant (|formantShift| > 0.5 st)              → LPC source-filter
  hasPitch AND SOLO AND voiced speech (2 kHz bp)    → LPC source-filter
  else                                               → Phase Vocoder
  (chord = BACKING content; its blocks were transiently passing the per-block
   2 kHz check and routing to LPC — SOLO gate fixed chord_Cmaj_pitch_up7st)
  LPC min guard order = 8 when voiced AND (hasFormant OR hasPitch) (else 2)

Pre-emphasis logic (Sessions 13–14, the v18 series):
  H(z) = 1 − 0.97z⁻¹ applied to LPC analysis frame; de-emphasis 1/(1−0.97z⁻¹)
  applied post-OLA (gated by anyUsePreEmph). Direction-aware bypass:
    formantUpBypass: voiced AND formant shift ≥ 0 AND NOT speech (2 kHz bp check)
      → use plain frame.  Pre-emph distorts upward pole shifts on pure tones
      (sine_440_formant_upmax: 32.8 plain vs 13.7 pre-emph) but helps downward
      (sine_440_formant_downmax: 29.9 pre-emph vs 25.4 plain).
    largePitchDown: voiced AND no formant shift AND pitch < −6 st
      → use plain frame.  Shifted F0 drops below pre-emph corner
      (vocal_aah_pitch_down12st: 27.0 plain vs 19.9 pre-emph); upward shifts
      benefit (vocal_aah_pitch_up7st: 24.9 pre-emph vs 19.0 plain).
  DO NOT fold de-emphasis into the LPC polynomial (A(z)×(1−0.97z⁻¹)): the
  product is order P+1 and truncating to P distorts ALL formant roots (v18d
  failure, scored 23.9–24.9).
