Pre-emphasis logic (Sessions 13-14, the v18 series):
  H(z) = 1 - 0.97z^-1 applied to LPC analysis frame. v18h: DUAL OLA STREAMS --
  pre-emphasised frames OLA into outputBuffer_ (de-emphasised continuously at
  read time); bypassed frames OLA into outputBufferPlain_ (read as-is); summed
  per sample at output. Direction-aware bypass:
    formantUpBypass: voiced AND formant shift >= 0 (v18h: now includes speech)
      -> use plain frame. Pre-emph distorts upward pole shifts
      (sine_440_formant_upmax: 32.8 plain vs 13.7 pre-emph, old metric;
      vocal_aah_formant_upmax recovered to 34.5 once dual streams removed the
      cross-tilt), but helps downward shifts -> keep pre-emph for those.
    largePitchDown: voiced AND no formant shift AND pitch < -6 st
      -> use plain frame (vocal_aah_pitch_down12st 27.0 plain vs 19.9 pre-emph,
      old metric); upward pitch keeps pre-emph (vocal_aah_pitch_up7st 24.9 vs 19.0).
    polyphonicContent (VariphraseParams flag, set by Hybrid routing for
      ENSEMBLE/BACKING): bypassed frames route into the DE-EMPH stream anyway --
      chord formant empirically wants the low-shelf tilt (21.2 tilted vs 17.9
      clean vs 19.8 pre-emph, old metric).
  DO NOT fold de-emphasis into the LPC polynomial (A(z)*(1-0.97z^-1)): order
  P+1 truncated to P distorts ALL formant roots (v18d failure).
  DO NOT de-emphasise per-frame with fresh state (v18 experiment, 27.0):
  the integrator needs cross-frame state at F0 ~ 120 Hz; dual streams with
  continuous state per stream is the working design.

F0 ESTIMATION (v19b/c, SourceFilterModel::estimateF0):
  - NO pre-emphasis in estimateF0 (v19c): it boosts formants and on speech the
    F1-region ACF peaks (~600 Hz) beat the fundamental — F0 flapped 130<->600 Hz
    and pitch shifts rendered a fourth sharp. ACF pitch detection wants the
    fundamental dominant (the opposite of LPC analysis needs).
  - frame reaching ACF is Hann^2-tapered (windowed twice); winAcf_ (ACF of w^2,
    precomputed in prepare) unbiases it. Correction clamped at 2.0 (diverges at
    long lags -> spurious peaks).
  - OCTAVE GUARD: only integer subharmonics of the global max (lag/2,/3,/4,
    +-2 samples) qualify as shorter-lag candidates; a looser "any local max
    within 5%" rule picked formant sub-peaks at non-integer fractions.
  - Verified output fundamentals: sine cases 441.0 Hz (target 440); vocal
    pitch+7 190.5 Hz (target 195.9).

EXCITATION (v20): FLAT harmonic weighting in synthesiseExcitation (band-limited
  impulse train). DO NOT use 1/k sawtooth weighting: the spectral tilt is
  already carried once per path (pre-emph path: output de-emphasis filter;
  bypass path: LPC envelope of the untilted frame) — 1/k double-counts it,
